# Data request: long-term brand model

<!-- Send to whoever owns the data. Delete rows that do not apply. -->

We are building a model of how brand marketing affects sales over several years. Please send the following, **weekly** where possible, for the longest period available (four years or more).

## Sales and brand metrics

| Item | Detail needed |
|---|---|
| Sales | Revenue or volume per week. If you have MMM "base sales", send total sales too and say exactly how base was built. |
| Awareness | Weekly score, question wording, sample size per week, supplier, any changes in method with dates. |
| Consideration / preference | As above. |
| Other brand metrics | Share of search, branded search volume, web visits: how computed, any smoothing. |

## Drivers

| Item | Detail needed |
|---|---|
| Media by channel | Spend in £ per week (and impressions or reach if available). **Every** brand activity, including PR, sponsorship, social and partnerships. |
| Performance media | Spend per week by channel. |
| Price and promotions | Average price, promotion weeks and depth. |
| Distribution | Any change in where the product is sold. |
| Competitors | Their media spend or share of voice if known. |
| Macro | Anything the business tracks that affects demand (interest rates, confidence). |

## Questions

1. How are brand budgets set? Are they planned ahead, or changed in response to recent sales or brand health?
2. Were there any launches or rebrands? Any outages, crises or big PR moments? Please list dates.
3. Did any measurement change (tracker supplier, survey wording, website analytics)? Please list dates.
4. What does one unit of each media variable cost (for example, spend in £1,000s)?
5. Is there an existing MMM? If so, which tool, and how is its baseline built?
